About AI Career Coach

We believe everyone deserves a clear, data-driven path to a career they love — not guesswork.

Our Mission

AI Personal Career Coach was built to remove the guesswork from career planning. Students and job seekers often don't know which roles truly fit their skills and interests, or what's missing between where they are and where they want to be. We use AI-driven similarity matching, structured skill-gap analysis, and personalized roadmaps to close that gap.

How Our AI Works

Our recommendation engine vectorizes your skills and interests, then compares them against a curated dataset of 25+ career profiles using cosine similarity — the same mathematical technique used in modern search and recommendation systems — to surface the roles that fit you best.
